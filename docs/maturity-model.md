## Purpose

The maturity model lets a team describe, honestly, how much of APES it actually uses. Levels are not for bragging. They are for diagnosing where adoption is weak and where to invest effort next.

## Levels (Placeholder)

A final five-level model (Ad Hoc → Repeatable → Defined → Managed → Optimized) will be defined in a later sprint. Until then, treat any self-assessment as provisional.

## How to Use

- Pick one representative project and walk through the lifecycle.
- For each stage, ask: "Do we have evidence, or do we have an opinion?"
- Identify the stage with the weakest evidence and focus the next sprint there.
