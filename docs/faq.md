## General

### Is APES an industry standard?

No. APES is an early-stage draft (v0.1). It is not an industry standard, and no adoption numbers have been verified.

### Does APES replace general product frameworks (Lean, Shape Up, etc.)?

No. APES composes with general product frameworks and engineering-quality standards. It does not replace them.

### Does APES favor any specific AI tool or vendor?

No. APES is vendor-neutral. Specific tools may appear in examples or guidance but are not required by the standard.

## Adoption

### What is the fastest way to start using APES?

Pick one active project and apply the Discovery and Validation stages before any code is written. Use the checklists under [`../checklists/`](../checklists/README.md) and record decisions in [`../governance/`](../governance/README.md).

### Do I have to adopt every stage at once?

No. APES modules are designed to be adopted incrementally. Start where you are weakest.

## Process

### Where do I propose a change to a stage?

Open a standard proposal issue using the [template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/standard-proposal.md), and consider drafting an RFC in [`../governance/rfc/`](../governance/rfc/README.md).

### Where do I document a cross-cutting decision?

In [`../governance/adr/`](../governance/adr/README.md) as an ADR.
