## Who This Is For

Builders, product managers, engineers, and researchers who want to apply APES to an AI-assisted product effort.

## First Reading Path

1. Read [`../README.md`](../README.md).
2. Read [`../VISION.md`](../VISION.md) and [`../MANIFESTO.md`](../MANIFESTO.md).
3. Read [`lifecycle.md`](lifecycle.md) and [`stage-gates.md`](stage-gates.md).
4. Skim [`glossary.md`](glossary.md).
5. Browse [`../standards/`](../standards/README.md) for the stage relevant to your current work.

## First Sprint Suggestion

To apply APES for the first time, start at the **Discovery** stage and move sequentially. Do not skip the validation stages even if the idea feels obvious — the goal is evidence, not confidence.

## Next Steps

- Pick a current project and tag which stage you are in.
- Use the relevant checklist under [`../checklists/`](../checklists/README.md).
- Capture any decision with cross-cutting impact as an ADR in [`../governance/adr/`](../governance/adr/README.md).
