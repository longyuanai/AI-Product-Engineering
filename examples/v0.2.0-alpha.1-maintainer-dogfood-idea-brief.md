## Opportunity

- Opportunity statement: Make the existing nine templates and nine gates usable through one short, explicit, tool-neutral path.
- Affected people or systems: First-time APES readers and the repository documentation they navigate.
- Observed context: At tracked baseline commit `0814e68d9fd9f6060e4be4f7072cc54c1788ad06`, Getting Started was a `Placeholder` reading path and the commands index stated that no command file existed (`LOCAL-005`).
- Why this may matter now: The Sprint 05 completion record reports nine templates and nine exit-gate checklists delivered (`LOCAL-006`). **Inference:** navigation and ownership are now the smallest remaining release-candidate gap; this inference has not been tested with an unaffiliated first-time user.

## Problem Hypothesis

- Hypothesis: If APES maps every stage to one template and exit gate, requires project-owned working copies, and names a human decision owner, then a first-time reader can produce one bounded artifact without editing canonical files or drifting into another stage.
- Assumptions: The reader can access the repository, choose a current stage, copy Markdown files, and provide evidence relevant to their project.
- Disconfirming observation: A reader cannot identify required paths or ownership from Getting Started and the command, modifies a canonical APES file, or reaches handoff without knowing who records the gate decision.

## Intended Outcomes

- Desired user or stakeholder outcome: A reader completes one stage artifact and a prepared gate record, then hands the decision to a named human.
- Candidate structural success criterion: Repository contracts pass for every documented stage/template/gate mapping, required input, copy-safety rule, decision-owner rule, and final stop.
- Internal dogfood criterion: Maintainer review finds no blocking ambiguity in the Idea walkthrough.
- Evidence boundary: The other eight stage paths and unaffiliated first-time-user usability remain unverified.
- Time horizon: One first-use session for the selected stage.

## Risks and Unknowns

- Highest-impact unknown: Whether an unaffiliated first-time user interprets the path consistently without maintainer help.
- Potential harm or misuse: A user may treat generated prose or checked boxes as evidence, or treat internal dogfood as external validation.
- Privacy, security, or compliance concern: Real project evidence may contain sensitive data; adopters must apply their own handling controls and minimize what enters artifacts.
- Earliest Discovery question: Can an external user complete one stage and explain the canonical/working-copy and agent/human decision boundaries without prompting?

## Out of Scope

- Solutions or features not being proposed: Standard module completion, automation, validator integration, publication tooling, or automatic gate decisions.
- Users, markets, or contexts not covered: External teams, regulated deployments, and non-Markdown workflows.
- Decisions intentionally deferred: External usability study, final v0.2 scope, F2 re-review, F3, OQ-11, and public release actions.
