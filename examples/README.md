## Purpose

Examples show what conformance to APES actually looks like across one or more stages. They are the primary mechanism for stress-testing the standard against real work.

## Selection Criteria

An example is admitted only when:

- It documents its scope, the stage(s) covered, and the evidence used.
- It cites the specific APES version it was validated against.
- It records what the standard failed to handle, not only what it succeeded at.
