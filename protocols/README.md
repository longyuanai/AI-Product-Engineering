## Purpose

This directory will house protocols — structured ways of working between people (and between people and AI agents) that complement the standard. Examples include sprint rituals, review processes, and handoff conventions.

## Relationship to Standards

Protocols describe *how* a stage operates day-to-day. Standards describe *what* must be true at the end of a stage. Both are needed; they are not interchangeable.
