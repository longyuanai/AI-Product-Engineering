## Working Copy Rule

Copy this canonical checklist to `GATE_RECORD_PATH` before use. Record evidence and criterion state only in that project-owned working copy; never mark or decide the canonical APES file. An agent may prepare the record, but only the human named by `GATE_DECISION_OWNER` records `Pass`, `Failed`, or `Deferred`.

## Required Evidence

- Direct observations are distinguishable from participant language and researcher interpretation.
- Each session or source has a date, relevant context, and data-handling constraint.
- Repeated patterns and counterexamples cite the observations that support them.
- The limits of participant or context coverage are explicit.

## Gate Checklist

- [ ] Discovery questions and assumptions were recorded before interpretation.
- [ ] Evidence entries include source and access date.
- [ ] Sensitive data is minimized and handling constraints are documented.
- [ ] Patterns cite supporting observations and name counter-evidence.
- [ ] Unsupported interpretations remain hypotheses.
- [ ] Focused Research questions and evidence needs are defined.

## Failure and Compensating Evidence

If the gate does not pass, record `Failed` or `Deferred`, affected questions, missing observations, an owner, and a review date. Scheduling future interviews without current evidence does not satisfy the gate.

Compensating evidence may include dated support records, field observations, usage data, or previously collected interviews that address the same context. It must have traceable provenance, a documented access date, and an explicit explanation of sampling or context limitations.
