## Purpose

APES does not exist in a vacuum. Notes here describe other product-engineering and AI-engineering standards, frameworks, and methodologies so that APES can complement them rather than duplicate them.

## Scope

In scope:

- Neutral descriptions of neighboring standards.
- Overlap and complementarity analyses.
- Compatibility notes.

Out of scope:

- Marketing-style comparisons.
- Unverified claims about the size or adoption of any alternative.
